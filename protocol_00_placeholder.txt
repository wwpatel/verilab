Serial Dilution Protocol (placeholder for pipeline testing)

1. Add 100 uL of PBS buffer to wells A1 through A12 of a 96-well flat-bottom plate.
2. Add 100 uL of stock sample to well A1 and mix by pipetting up and down 3 times.
3. Transfer 100 uL from well A1 to well A2 and mix by pipetting up and down 3 times.
4. Repeat the transfer and mix step serially from A2 to A3, A3 to A4, continuing through A11 to A12.
5. Discard 100 uL from well A12 after mixing so all wells contain equal final volume.
6. Incubate the plate at room temperature for 10 minutes before reading.
