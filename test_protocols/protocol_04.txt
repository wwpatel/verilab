Mix reagents

1. In a sterile 10 mL reservoir, add 2534 uL of Platinum Hot Start PCR 2X Master Mix and 3295 uL of PCR Clean Water per set of 384 samples. Mix by pipetting.

Setup epMotion automation platform
2. Place a box of 1-50 uL epT.I.P.S. w/filters on deck slot A4. Place empty 384 PCR Primer Plates 1-3 on deck slots B1-B3. Place 10 mL reservoir with PCR Master Mix in the first slot of the reservoir rack, then place reservoir rack on deck slot B4.

Execute automated protocol
3. Remove box lids and execute protocol (protocol must be imported to epBlue software prior to execution; epBlue 4.6 or later). The automated protocol transfers 4.6 uL of PCR Master Mix into three 384-well PCR plates using the multidispense feature of the epBlue software.
