ZymoBIOMICS 96 MagBead DNA Extraction (Opentrons Flex)

Starting with 200 uL of lysed sample in a deep-well block on the Heater-Shaker with deepwell adapter.

1. Resuspend beads in source labware with pipette (aspirate/dispense).
2. Add 625 uL ZymoBIOMICS MagBinding Buffer (already containing the MagBinding Beads).
3. Mix well on heater-shaker for 10 minutes.
4. Transfer the 96-deepwell plate to a magnetic block until beads pellet.
5. Aspirate and discard the supernatant.
6. Remove the plate from the magnetic block.
7. Dispense 500 uL of ZymoBIOMICS MagBinding Buffer and mix well by pipette or shaker plate for 1 minute.
8. Transfer the plate to the magnetic block until beads pellet.
9. Aspirate and discard the supernatant.
10. Remove the plate from the magnetic block.
11. Dispense 500 uL of ZymoBIOMICS MagWash 1 and mix well by pipette or shaker plate for 1 minute.
12. Transfer the plate to the magnetic block until beads pellet.
13. Aspirate and discard the supernatant.
14. Remove the plate from the magnetic block.
15. Dispense 900 uL ZymoBIOMICS MagWash 2 and mix well by pipette or shaker plate for 1 minute.
16. Transfer the plate to a magnetic block until beads pellet.
17. Aspirate and discard the supernatant.
18. Remove the plate from the magnetic block.
19. Repeat the MagWash 2 wash step (option to repeat twice for a total of 3 washes, from a 2 mL NEST deepwell on deck slot B3).
20. Transfer the plate onto a heating element at 55C until beads dry, approximately 10 minutes.
21. Dispense 50 uL of ZymoBIOMICS DNase/RNase Free Water to each well and resuspend beads (elution volume adjustable 10-100 uL).
22. Mix the beads well for 10 minutes.
23. Transfer the plate onto the magnetic block for 2-3 minutes until beads pellet.
24. Transfer the supernatant, containing the eluted DNA, to a clean elution plate.

Runtime parameters: Dry Run (On/Off, default Off), Elution Volume (10-100 uL, default 50), Additional wash (On/Off, default Off).
