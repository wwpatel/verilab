Make PCR Master Mix for 100 samples in 15 mL Falcon tube.

2. Add 850 uL PCR Water (nuclease free)
3. Dilute SYBR G 1:100 by adding 3 uL SYBR G and 297 uL PCR water to a new small tube. Vortex and spin down.
4. Add 250 uL of SYBR 1:100 dilution to Falcon tube
5. Add 100 uL of Primer 1: GOH-279-M13 10uM to Falcon tube
6. Add 100 uL of Primer 2: GOH-280-M13 10uM to Falcon tube
7. Add 1000 uL of 5' mix to Falcon tube
8. Quick vortex and spin down Falcon tube
9. Label new green 96 microplate
10. Add 23 uL of Master Mix to each well of green 96 microplate
11. Thaw and spin down plate with extracted DNA samples.
12. Add 2 uL of each extracted DNA sample to corresponding well of new plate with master mix.
13. Seal PCR plate with plastic film, spin down
14. Run plate on BioRad CFX with hsp60 qPCR 25 uL 40 cycle program.
