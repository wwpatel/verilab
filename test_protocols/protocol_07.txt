24. After centrifugation at 2000 x g for 3 min, the supernatant was carefully discarded, and the DNA pellet was air dried at room temperature for 10 min or until the pellet got glassy.

25. Addition of 0.5 mL DNA Hydration Solution (Qiagen) for a large pellet or 0.3 mL for a smaller pellet was followed by incubation at 65 °C in a warming cabinet for 1 hour. To fully dissolve the DNA, the sample was put on a shaker overnight at room temperature.

26. Samples were centrifuged briefly and the dissolved DNA was transferred to a 2 mL tube and stored at -20 °C.
